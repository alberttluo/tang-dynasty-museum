Ceramics

Sancai — "three colours" — is a lead-fluxed glaze system that runs during firing. The colours are not painted on so much as allowed to move, and the finished surface is a record of what happened in the kiln. Zoom in and the technique becomes legible.

The room runs from the polychrome tomb figures of the Xuanzong decades to the monochrome celadons that followed the rebellion of 755, because that is the order in which Tang potters changed their minds about what a fine pot was.
